Artist Biography

HEKON, whose full name is Akinde Olawale Ebenezer, is a Nigerian music artist born in Idanre Local Government Area, Ondo State, Nigeria.

His musical journey began in 2013 when he started singing as a choir member at HIS GRACE EVANGELICAL MINISTRY.

Through music, HEKON expresses emotions, personal experiences, life challenges and stories that connect with listeners.

Today, HEKON continues to develop his musical career and share his sound with audiences around the world.
