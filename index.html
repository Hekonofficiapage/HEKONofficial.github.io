About HEKON

HEKON, whose full name is Akinde Olawale Ebenezer, is a Nigerian music artist and songwriter. Through music, HEKON expresses emotions, personal experiences, relationships, and the realities of life. His goal is to connect with listeners through meaningful songs and authentic storytelling.
